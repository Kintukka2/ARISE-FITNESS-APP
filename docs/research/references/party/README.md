# The party

References for the party's overhaul (the owner's call: the brief's board, its bonuses and its hint were a whole screen
inside one section of the brief). Not a step of the order to build in
[docs/DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md#order-to-build). No captures are kept for this one. The screens are described
from the games and from the pages below, and nothing in ARISE copies their art.

## How the games lay a team before a fight

**Honkai: Star Rail** keeps the team as a strip of four faces in a corner of the field, and a tap on it opens Team Setup
at any moment, not only from a menu. The setup lays the four out large, with a Quick Setup to fill them, and where a
character stands in the line matters. ([GamesRadar's guide](https://www.gamesradar.com/au/honkai-star-rail-guide/),
[iTech Post on its quality-of-life changes](https://www.itechpost.com/articles/117644/20230517/5-qol-improvements-hoyoverse-added-honkai-star-rail.htm),
[Icy Veins on team building](https://www.icy-veins.com/honkai-star-rail/team-building))
- ARISE took the strip on the brief: one row of faces that opens everything about the party.

**AFK Arena** deploys its heroes in a front row and a back row facing the enemy, and sets the faction bonus the line
reaches as one mark beside it, tapped for its tiers. The bonus reaches every hero in the formation, not only the
faction's own. ([AFK Arena Wiki on factions](https://afk-arena.fandom.com/wiki/Faction),
[Gamer Empire's faction guide](https://gamerempire.net/afk-arena-faction-advantages-guide/))
- ARISE took the formation's two rows, as its board already had them, and the bonus as a mark: lit hexes on the
  brief's row, and a card to each in the window.

**Solo Leveling: Arise** redrew its Battle Tier view so the combat power of the hunter, the three hunters beside them,
the Shadows and the gear stand in one place.
([dotgg's notes on its update](https://dotgg.gg/solo-leveling-arise/9-25-thu-update-details/),
[Sportskeeda's Army of Shadows guide](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/solo-leveling-arise-army-shadows-guide-how-unlock-use))
- ARISE took the party's power split by who brings it: the hunter's, the Association's hunters' and the Shadows'.

**Genshin Impact** states its Elemental Resonance at the head of its party setup once two of an element stand in it:
the element's mark and what it gives the whole party.
- ARISE took a card to each role bonus and Kin the squad reaches, with what it gives, and under them what every member
  gains.

**Darkest Dungeon** lines its party up before it embarks, each hero in the rank they will fight from, and lists each
one's numbers on their own panel.

**Magia Record, Seven Knights and Tokyo Beast** keep a team screen that previews the formation and its leader, offers an
automatic formation to start from, and states what a combination gives.
([GamePress on Magia Record's formation](https://gamepress.gg/magiarecord/node/2651),
[GameWith on Seven Knights Re:Birth](https://gamewith.net/sevenknights-rebirth/70388),
[Tokyo Beast's party help](https://open-2c.gitbook.com/url/tokyogamestoken.gitbook.io/tokyobeast-help/trials/battle-menu/party/how-to-party))
- ARISE took Auto Deploy beside the Army at the window's foot.

## What ARISE takes

- **On the brief, one row.**
  - The Front's faces and the Rear's across a seam, an empty Shadow slot as a dashed square.
  - The bonuses the squad reaches as lit marks, and a chevron.
  - The whole row is the button.
- **The window:**
  - The party's power split by who brings it.
  - The formation, a face dragged or tapped across.
  - A card to each bonus, with its tooltip.
  - A row to each member with its four numbers, what the squad adds in green, and a tap into its card.
  - The Army and Auto Deploy.
